## 4 Core Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations

### Insight 1: Content Licensing Waste vs Engagement ROI
* **Observation**: 22% of licensed movie titles account for less than 3% of total watch hours despite representing $1.8M in annual licensing expenses.
* **Business Impact**: Significant capital is locked in non-performing content inventory.
* **Recommendation**: Implement a dynamic content licensing score threshold (Cost per Watch Hour < $0.45) for contract renewals.
* **Expected Outcome**: Reallocate $1.2M in content acquisition budget toward high-performing regional original series.

### Insight 2: Buffering Threshold as a Primary Churn Driver
* **Observation**: Subscribers experiencing >= 3 buffering events per session demonstrate a 42% churn rate within 30 days.
* **Business Impact**: Network performance degradation directly degrades subscriber retention and MRR.
* **Recommendation**: Deploy real-time CDN switching and automatic bitrate fallback algorithms for mobile network users.
* **Expected Outcome**: Reduce technical churn by 18%, saving $350K in monthly subscription cancellations.
